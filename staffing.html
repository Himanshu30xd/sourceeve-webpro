Connecting Global Talent with Global Opportunities

SourceEve Staffing Solutions is the recruitment and workforce solutions division of SourceEve Group of Companies. We help businesses identify, source, and recruit suitable talent across multiple industries and international markets.

Our services cover blue-collar and white-collar positions, from general workers and skilled tradespeople to technical professionals, administrative staff, and other qualified personnel.

Our sourcing network focuses on India, Africa, Sri Lanka, Nepal, the Philippines, Vietnam, and other potential markets. Our philosophy is simple: the right talent, the right opportunity, and the right connection.

Our recruitment services

  • Overseas recruitment and international manpower sourcing
  • Blue-collar and skilled workforce recruitment
  • White-collar and professional recruitment
  • Bulk hiring and project-based recruitment
  • Employer-led recruitment drives and interview coordination
  • Candidate screening and shortlisting
  • Recruitment coordination and mobilization support
  • Recruitment partnership solutions for employers and authorized agencies
