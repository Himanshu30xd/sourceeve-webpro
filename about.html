About SourceEve Group of Companies

Building connections. Creating opportunities. Expanding horizons.

SourceEve Group of Companies is a diversified business group focused on international recruitment, import and export, and event management and exhibition solutions.

Our vision is to build a trusted international business network that connects companies with talent, markets with products, and brands with opportunities. Through our specialized sister companies, we deliver focused, practical, and professional solutions tailored to the evolving needs of businesses across industries and regions.

Our Vision

To become a trusted international group recognized for connecting global talent, facilitating trade, and delivering memorable business experiences.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, customer-focused business solutions through specialized services, ethical practices, international collaboration, and a commitment to long-term value creation.

Our Core Values

Integrity

Building trust through transparency and responsible business practices.

Excellence

Delivering professional, practical, and quality-focused solutions.

Global Mindset

Connecting markets, talent, and opportunities across borders.

Partnership

Creating long-term relationships built around shared success.

A Global Outlook, Built on Local Connections

Through our recruitment, international trade, and events and exhibitions divisions, we aim to connect businesses and opportunities across Asia, Africa, the Middle East, Europe, Australia, and North America.

Our approach combines international reach with an understanding of local requirements, market expectations, and the importance of dependable partnerships.
